Today I ran a demo day for the open source CRMs on my shortlist. Five tools, one afternoon, and a rule that I could only click through each one for twenty minutes. It was tiring, but it showed me which products I would actually want to use for a week.

{{image: crm-demo-day | The five CRM demos, each with the same sample contacts loaded}}

The format

  • Same sample dataset in every tool: 40 contacts, 12 companies, and 8 open deals
  • Same three tasks: add a new lead, move a deal to the next stage, and find everything about one company
  • Same stopwatch, and a note on anything that felt slow or confusing

Notes from each demo

  • Twenty: the fastest for moving deals, and the timeline made the company lookup obvious
  • SuiteCRM: everything I needed was there, but I had to dig to find the lead conversion path
  • EspoCRM: quick to change a field layout, and the lists stayed responsive with the sample data
  • Frappe CRM: the cleanest pipeline screen, and calls were easy to log against a deal
  • Krayin: simple and clear, though the reporting was thin for the lookup task

What surprised me

The biggest surprise was how much the "find everything about one company" task separated the tools. The ones that put a timeline on the company page answered it in seconds. The others needed three or four clicks across different modules.

What I'd do differently

I would have included a task around reporting, since that is where the heavier suites pull ahead. I would also have had a second person run the demos, so I was not the only one judging what felt intuitive.

Next step

I am narrowing the list to two tools for a proper week-long trial. The full write-ups are in the reviews, and the summary is in our open source CRM comparison.
