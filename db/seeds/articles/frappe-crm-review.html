Frappe CRM is the newest entry in this comparison, and it is the one with the cleanest sales workflow. Built by the team behind the Frappe framework and ERPNext, it focuses on leads, deals, and the calls and emails that move them forward. If your team lives in the pipeline, this is worth a look.

What Frappe CRM is

Frappe CRM runs on the Frappe framework, which is a Python back end with a Vue.js front end. That means it sits on the same foundation as ERPNext, so it can share customers and items with an accounting or inventory setup if you already use one. It can also run on its own.

Setup

Installation is the most involved of the five. You create a "bench", install the Frappe framework, and then add the CRM app to a site. The official documentation is good, and the process is repeatable once you have done it once.

  • Use a Linux server with Python, Node, Redis, and MariaDB available
  • Create a site per environment so you can test upgrades before touching production
  • Enable the scheduler and background workers, since emails and integrations rely on them

What I liked

  • Pipeline first. Leads convert to deals with a single action, and deal pages keep the next step visible at the top.
  • Calls and emails. Call logs and email threads are attached to the right lead or deal, which keeps the timeline honest.
  • Modern interface. The Vue front end is fast and uncluttered. New reps needed only a short walkthrough.
  • Ecosystem. Because it is part of the Frappe family, it can connect to accounting, inventory, and HR apps when you need them.

Where it falls short

  • Narrower feature set. Marketing campaigns and service case management are lighter than in SuiteCRM or EspoCRM.
  • Setup complexity. The bench-based install is harder for a team with no Python or Linux experience. Budget time, or use a hosting provider that offers it as a one-click app.
  • Smaller plugin community. You will find fewer ready-made integrations than for the older tools.

Who should use Frappe CRM

Frappe CRM is ideal for a sales team that wants a focused pipeline tool and plans to connect it to ERP or accounting later. It is less suited to marketing-heavy teams or organizations that need a long list of built-in service modules.

Verdict

Frappe CRM is a strong, focused choice. The sales workflow is the best in this group, and the path to a wider ERP setup is a real advantage. The setup is heavier than Twenty or Krayin, so plan for it. Compare it with the other four options in our open source CRM comparison.
